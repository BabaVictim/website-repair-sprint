Before you start

A few useful
details.

What does “one page” include?+

A single scrolling page with up to five sections. For example: an introduction, your offer, key benefits, FAQs, and a contact section. Navigation can jump to sections on that same page.

When does the 24-hour window start?+

After your brief has been reviewed and accepted, the scope is agreed, and the required content and assets are available. It covers the first draft. Revision and final handoff timing are agreed separately.

Can I use my own logo and writing?+

Yes. Supply up to 800 words, one logo, and up to five images you have permission to use, plus your preferred colors. Light editing for clarity is included; a full brand identity or long-form copywriting project is outside this package.

Will my website include a contact form?+

Your page can link to your email, calendar, or an existing form service. A custom form backend, payment system, or other server-side feature would need a separately agreed scope.

How do I book or pay?+

Prepare a brief for yourself, then use the booking contact to discuss the project. GitHub requests are public, so share only a public summary there. Scope, payment arrangements, and a private route for any required assets are agreed before work begins. There is no payment collection on this website.
