01 / START WHERE YOU ARE

Less pressure.
More perspective.

A new direction doesn't have to mean starting over.

Maybe you've outgrown a role. Maybe you're returning after a break. Or maybe something feels a little off, and you haven't found the words for it yet.

This concept puts a calm conversation at the center: noticing what matters, making sense of the options, and choosing a next step that feels like yours.
